# Moonshot

## Goal

Pick one moonshot direction for the plugin, ignoring developer effort: compactions that keep what the work needs, so a compaction stops costing us in the middle of feature work, without spending more money. How often they happen matters only through what it costs.

## Decided

- Test the raw tail size first, offline, on the transcripts we have: "instead of waiting a week".
- Frequent compactions are fine: "i dont mind frequent compactions at all if its keeping the details it needs. it also keeps our models in their smart zone". What matters: "the compaction needs to contain what is necessary".
- The pages are the direction to test next, offline, with Ember building them: "yes". They are Anthropic's dreaming moved inside the session; dreaming across sessions comes later, for a session that starts cold.
- Bring the Ember benchmark into the main checkout: "ok" (done, uncommitted). The 15k floor was approved in the same "ok" and is on hold for the cost measurement below.
- Improve the benchmark from the review: "based on the fable review, can you improve the benchmark?". The size gates leave the quality total; the verdict keeps cost as its second axis ("better" needs cost within 10%).
- Cost is a constraint: "More frequent compactions means we invalidate the cache more often. If compacting more often means we end up spending more money, that is something I do not want. There is a sweet spot." This replaces "i dont mind frequent compactions at all": frequency is fine only where it does not cost more.
- Code is written by Opus at medium effort: "write all code with opus medium".
- The benchmark moves to Ember, the model the plugin uses: "needs to be made to work with ember, not pi and luna". The Observer path and the `--behavior` reader both move; results recorded with Luna stop being comparable.

## Rejected

- Faster or cheaper compaction events: already 0.5 s and about $0.002 per Observer call.
- Distilling tool output as it is produced: large results are too small a share (25% of result tokens), and arXiv 2607.12161 found a 38% token cut raised billed cost 6.8%.
- More Observer prompt work, a reflector, a `<state>` page that replaces the log: already failed (`docs/WHAT-WE-TRIED.md`).
- A byte-stable head on the memory message for the prompt cache (part 3 of the proposal): the engine marks only two system blocks and the last message block, and a plugin cannot place a marker. Over 237 compactions the cache read after one never passed 9.4k; a memory message sharing 87% with the previous one read the same as one sharing 3%.
- Pages that Ember fills ahead of time by pointing at lines (parts 1 and 2 of the proposal, with Ember as editor): it cannot tell which lines will be wanted. 94% of what it picks is never targeted, and no budget lifts it past 39% of targets, under a plain 30k tail. Not tried: another prompt, the main model as editor.
- Stopping growth at the source: repeats of injected text are 0.6% of growth and hook output 1.3%; cutting every tool result to 2k saves 6.6%. Growth is the model's own output and many ordinary Bash results.
- Project memory across sessions (a runbook and map built from a project's past transcripts; `~/work/scratch/om-analysis/cross-session-report.md`, 33 projects, 656 sessions, no model): a session repeats little of what earlier sessions held. Project commands by whole core 22.1% against 5.5% by chance, most of it one project's helper scripts; lookups of Bash targets 10.6% against 5.4% (n=179); orientation tokens in the first 30 requests already held 6.3% against 1.6%; errors seen before 1.3%. A median 1 recurring project command per project. Limit: exact matching undercounts, and most file reading goes through `cat`, `sed` and `rg`, so file-level recurrence is not well measured.
- Compacting at a seam (the first turn end past 120k where work is at rest, else 157k as today; `~/work/scratch/om-analysis/seam-report.md`, 150 sessions, 387 compactions, no model): both kill criteria met. Rework in the 30 tool calls after a compaction at a seam against not at one: +0.74 [-0.52, 2.10], with `om_recall` +0.83 and re-reads +1.03 the wrong way; no single signal does better (committed -0.04, clean -0.40, green +1.54). The continuity split, the most a perfect signal could reach, is -0.47 [-1.27, 0.37]. Only 28% of cycles that reached 120k had a seam at an earlier turn end (43% counting the compaction's own), and 34% had no turn end in the window at all. The rule would make about 7% more compactions. Limits: signals are regex over Bash text; no session used a task list; the compaction's kind confounds (typed ones come at a median 116k, auto at 200k).
- Ember as the picker of which observations to keep (`~/work/scratch/om-analysis/dream-test-report.md`, 80 compactions, 33 sessions, 12,480 carried lines, 531 calls, $0.79). Obsolete labels (superseded, process, duplicate): 10.7% of carried characters against a 20% bar, a median 2.1k tokens per compaction; lines so labelled are used from memory at 60% of the live rate (superseded 8.6% against live 10.7%), and only 64.5% of droppable labels repeat on a second run. Relevance pick given the kept tail and continuation: marks 75% of carried characters not needed (median 10k tokens) but keeps only 28% of the lines the agent then uses, a tie with keeping lines that share a term with the tail (26%). Correctness of 300 carried lines, judged by Ember itself: 75.7% supported, 21.7% not found in the source span, 2.7% contradicted. At Ember's max effort the relevance pick is no better (80 compactions, $0.40, median 11.7 s a call): 80.5% marked not needed, 26.1% of used lines kept, against 23.3% for the tail-term baseline; low and max agree on 79% of lines. Limits: use is term matching, so silent use is invisible; no prune was replayed.
- The main model class as the picker (10 Opus subagents, one per compaction, each grading the carried lines 0 to 3 from the continuation and a rendering of the cycle's transcript; 2,468 carried lines, 416 used from memory; `dream-test-report.md` section 7): under the bar of about 70% of used lines kept at half the carried characters dropped. Limits: a subagent with a rendering (thinking left out), not a real fork with the whole context; use is term matching; 10 compactions chosen for having many used lines.
- Truncation at the threshold: parked for its cold cache rewrite (`tool-output-truncation.md`).
