# ACON: Observer prompt tuned from mined failures

Verdict: **PARK**. Both rounds gained on the main corpus but lost on the holdout. The stop rule ended the run after round 2.

## Method

- Split: tune on the main corpus (3 sessions, 25 cuts), test on the holdout (`tests/fixtures/holdout.local.json`: 3 sessions, 14 cuts, mechanical gates only). Main's two holdout draws differ by 0.3, so a +2 gain there would stand out.
- The bench grades no raw reference for retention. A failure counts when every needle of the failed item is in an Observer chunk before the cut and in none of the Observer's replies (`observed-missing`). Main v1.12.0, draws 1 and 2, all from cache.
- Three diagnosis agents read the 30 unique core failures (chunk excerpt plus reply), wrote the line the Observer should have written, and proposed general rules. No corpus names or figures went into the prompt.
- A/B: `bench-compact.ts ab --a main --b . --behavior --continuation --record --samples 2 --no-chain`, holdout on.

## Failures mined (main, 2 draws)

`observed-missing` item-cuts: chronology 183, identifiers 136, rationale 44, facts 35, multi-hop 25, lessons 18, decisions 14, file-state 12. Unique core items: 30. Their clusters:

- 15: the line says a report, plan or decision happened but drops its content and reason.
- 10: an exact name, label, log line or count is paraphrased.
- 3: a read file or tool output is recorded as "read X" without what it said.
- 2: dropped or ruled-out alternatives are lost.
- Cross-cutting: lines spent on bookkeeping (acks, monitor pings, version lists, cleanup).

## Edits

- Round 1: record what a report, plan, decision or read says, with the reason and the deciding value. Record dropped approaches and why. Quote names, labels and log lines from any source, and copy counts as written. Fold bookkeeping into one line.
- Round 2, from round 1's contrastive losses: the fold keeps the files, commands and ids it names (holdout status-file paths were lost). Never complete or guess an identifier (round 1 invented a commit hash).

- Round 1: main +0.9; rationale +7 to +24. Holdout −2.1.
- Round 2: main +2.0 (ab verdict B; lessons, decisions and multi-hop up). Holdout −1.6.
- Both rounds, holdout draw 2: the Observer invented the commit hash `9f1fa7b`. It is in neither the transcript nor any Observer input. It cost 5 cuts of the `absent` gate. Main never wrote it. The round 2 rule against guessed identifiers did not stop it.
- Without the invented hash, the holdout is still flat to slightly down: the tokens gate fell about 2 points as contexts grew.
- Size: Observer output +13% on the main corpus (≈81k to ≈92k tokens), +10% on the holdout. Mean context tokens +6% (22.9k to 24.5k).

## Why park

The gains sit in the hand-authored gates (lessons, decisions, rationale, multi-hop) and in behavior probes, all graded only on the main corpus the edits were mined from. The holdout has only mechanical gates, and it did not improve. It lost on size and once on a hallucinated hash. The goal needs ≥ +2 on held-out data in both draws.

## If picked up again

- The main-corpus gain is real enough to retest on a held-out split that has hand-authored items: mine from two main sessions, test on the third.
- Find out why the "record what a report says" rule makes the Observer write a sync hash it never saw.
